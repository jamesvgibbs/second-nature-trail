# Inhabited neighborhood and filter chapter review

November 18, 2026. This is the design and developer-review ledger for the new overview and representative filter interaction. It preserves the user-owned `visual-acceptance.md` and earlier evidence. The current authorized work is frontend presentation and interaction; the authoritative work orders, chapter schedule, supplies, scoring, shared world and saved games retain their existing contracts.

## Vision and diagnosis

The neighborhood should read as homes people occupy: a threshold connects to a clear street, a resident has a concrete problem, and a successful repair visibly changes that home. The filter chapter should put the inspected equipment and the available choices in that setting, with readable controls that work by keyboard or pointer. The previous overview had short disconnected doorstep strips and repeated tree placement; the previous filter flow largely presented the repair as a generic choice dialog. Different building silhouettes alone do not fix either problem.

## Implemented scene specification

The filter canvas remains 1200 × 800 on a four-pixel drawing grid, fitting the existing 3:2 display. Its main home occupies `(612,200,480,284)` and service cabinet `(632,368,152,172)`. Three choice cards are 220 × 164 at `(456,596)`, `(688,596)` and `(920,596)`. These are semantic choice hit areas, not walk targets. The resident stands at `(980,464)`, the player at `(412,512)`, and three companion anchors at `(92,700)`, `(164,720)`, `(236,700)`, clear of the home, equipment and cards. Native review determines readability at scaled sizes; the full authored options remain accessible as DOM buttons.

The palette declares ink `#203536`, sky `#bcc9b1`, distance `#81958c`, ground `#789170`, grass `#8fa37a`, shade `#647f65`, paper `#f3e9ca`, timber `#80654b`, wood `#aa875d`, roof `#304e50`, wall `#657773`, amber `#d29a55`, mint `#b6d18f`, dust `#a99e81`, dark `#465c57` and white `#e5ddbd`. The player also retains the existing palette choice. Static pixel primitives have one frame; there is no new animation, atlas, music or sound effect. The existing frame loop is unchanged; no measured FPS claim follows.

Scene states are inspect → decide → accepted installation → next home, then complete. Evidence/options are empty until the actual inspection. Cards preserve the public authored option IDs, including direction and replacement decisions. Accepted installation shows a clean frame, airflow marks, warm windows and resident acknowledgment; the previous home stays visible until Next. A six-home ledger derives directly from saved progress. Local installation snapshots require the current projection epoch and matching accepted step; they disappear on restart while the authoritative ledger remains. Shared restoration is separately derived from event contributions and its denominator.

Overview front walks join both homes above their nameplates, then branch through the actual label gap to the horizontal street; southern lots turn north between buildings. The first district aligns its walk with the existing Move Day crossing. Walkable centerlines begin at `doorY−12`; the decorative eight-pixel threshold above that point joins the facade without changing collision geometry. Three deterministic tree silhouettes retain the existing maximum 36 × 44 footprint. Sixteen small yard prop types fit their declared 38/42 × 14 rectangles. Final paint tests verify street/label/walk clearance and thresholds painted above foundation shadows.
