# Full visual rollout: design and verification

November 18, 2026. Jim authorized extending the inhabited, playable scene treatment through chapters 3–8, finale and shared surfaces. This ledger covers the authorized local implementation and developer verification. It preserves the existing user-owned `visual-acceptance.md`, historical screenshots and saved events. Hosting is not part of this work. No new approval gate is introduced.

## Vision and design delta

Each chapter should place the player beside a recognizable resident or neighborhood system, make the existing decision legible in that place, and show the consequence of an accepted action. The six remaining chapters must differ in their work, not just their titles: verify a notice, document damage, respond to a warning, manage a payment cycle, earn and spend a reward, and connect a network. Shared restoration remains visible without pretending another participant completed the current player’s work. Entry, practice, breaks, results, host and presentation should belong to the same visual world.

The original overview and the chapter-1/2 scenes are the established reference. Existing Canvas/DOM architecture, 1200 × 800 coordinates, responsive fit, player palette, safe replacement, reduced motion, controls, real-time multiplayer and saved state remain. New scene coordinates are semantic controls/illustrations; they do not alter authoritative movement coordinates or collision. Static sprites are acceptable; no animation is required to understand a cue. No cabinet bezel, coin/lives system, shooting mechanic, heavy CRT filter, engine migration, new scarcity, random penalty or automatic audio is introduced. Palette/sprite geometry and scene IDs will be recorded from the implemented renderer rather than invented in this plan. The existing frame-loop target is unchanged; a measured FPS claim requires measurements.

## Scene and shared-surface contracts

- **Focused scenes:** active chapter and paused-chapter context only, with a clear route back to the unchanged overview. Keep the player, current task, relevant supplies and next action identifiable at laptop size. At most three stable nearby companions, deliberate names only; all peers remain in Neighbors. Selected options must survive unrelated peer/SSE updates.
- **Accepted feedback:** distinguish inspection, incorrect attempt, partial work, completed site and completed objective. Show the accepted effect in place. Pending input is disabled and cannot submit twice. A newer phase/chapter/epoch/panel takes precedence over an older callback; Help, trade or settings focus must not be stolen. Reload/reconnect rebuild personal and shared state from projections.
- **Keyboard and pointer:** semantic hotspots and DOM controls send the same IDs/choices/paths. Number keys, configured interact keys, target arrows/Space and ordinary Tab/Enter must work from the controls that actually own focus. No essential action depends on dragging, hovering, speed or holding two keys. Graph links, warning markers and state cues remain available as text, not color alone.
- **Entry/setup/practice:** art before the insect choice is safe. Preserve invalid invitation/device/role handling, the required explicit visual choice, all five real practice steps, Ready, late join and countdown. Character preview cannot silently change the chosen avatar or palette.
- **Breaks/results:** preserve next-chapter preview, ranked/skipped information, actual standings, authoritative ending, unawarded explanations and unique prize allocation. Long names must not overwrite controls or map landmarks.
- **Host/presentation:** use safe visuals in every phase. Presentation receives only its existing read-only public projection; it must not borrow host roster, invitations, hidden answers or operational controls to populate decorative scenes. Host confirmations/reasons, owner/connection status, pause/advance/rescue/skip/end/close and export remain functional. Existing host authentication must survive opening the safe display.
- **Reduced motion/sound:** all progress and warnings remain understandable with reduced motion and silent audio. Preserve saved preferences and immediate replacement override. No new asset can bypass safe-mode selection or presentation policy.

## Scope of the conclusion

The implementation and developer checks can establish that the scenes preserve the existing rules and work through the observed inputs. They do not establish employee first-use comprehension, final benefit-copy approval, room-display readability, lowest-agreed-device FPS or representative-network performance. Those are post-build human/platform reviews, not reasons to leave discovered implementation defects unfinished. No previous screenshot certifies a new revision. All listed native recaptures are now observed. The final combined result and public-restoration correction are verified automatically; the final native presentation recaptures are recorded below.
