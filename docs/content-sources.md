# Content review sources

Checked 2026-11-09. These sources support the review process; they do not constitute SN approval of the game's questions or benefit claims.

Second Nature's [resident experience page](https://www.secondnature.com/solutions/residents) describes credit building and rewards for on-time payments, a renters insurance program, on-demand pest control, identity protection, air filter delivery, and group-rate internet. The game deliberately makes no numerical savings, insurance coverage, eligibility, or credit-score promises. Reviewers should confirm the package-specific wording before approval.

The answer-key review sheet is [content-review.md](content-review.md). Names, explanations, and answer keys must be approved together. The questions currently emphasize resident problems, safe usage, and misconceptions; an SN reviewer should assess whether they teach enough about the actual benefit workflow, especially moving support, credit reporting, rewards redemption, and internet setup.

Do not copy property-manager marketing claims into the game as universal terms. Actual package availability and service terms may differ. All game insurance, budgets, and credit outcomes remain fictional.
